Check regulator information
===========================

   .. note:: This content is only applicable to QCS6490.


   To check the regulator status and the voltage, run the following command:

   ::

      cat /sys/kernel/debug/regulator/regulator_summary



            .. flat-table:: 
               :header-rows: 0
               :class: longtable table-wrap

               * - :rspan:`1` 
                 - **DP0**
                 - Supports 3840 × 2160 at 60 fps 30 bpp
               * - **DP1**
                 - Supports 3840 × 2160 at 60 fps 30 bpp
               * - **Maximum concurrency**
                 - Supports 3840 × 2160 at 60 fps 30 bpp with DP0 as Primary + 3840 × 2160 at 60 fps 30 bpp with DP1 as Secondary
